Elliott Pursuit

A daily learning game for kids,
inspired by Trivial Pursuit.

How it works
📖
Learn one fact per category, every day Six categories unlock each day — Animals, Art, Numbers, Stories, The World, and Music. Tap to read the fact, then mark it learned.
🎯
Quiz yourself to earn pie slices After learning, answer a quiz question for each category. Get it right and you earn that slice of your pie.
🥧
Fill the pie, earn a prize Collect all 6 slices to complete a full pie. Earn 10 full pies and unlock a real-life reward — you decide what it is!
Features
🎓
Grade-level learning NEW Facts are tailored to your child's grade — Pre-K through 3rd Grade — with vocabulary and complexity that grows with them.
👨‍👩‍👧
One account for the whole family Add multiple children to a single account. Each child gets their own progress, streak, and facts.
🔥
Streaks & stats Track daily streaks, quiz accuracy, a calendar of every day played, and a full list of every fact ever learned.
🔀
Big Random Quiz Review everything your child has ever learned in a shuffled quiz — great for a car ride or rainy afternoon.

Elliott Pursuit is invite-only. You'll need an invite code from a friend or family member to create an account.
